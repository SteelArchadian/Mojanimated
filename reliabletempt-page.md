**Reliable Tempt** makes animal tempting reliable, for Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge.

- Animals **start following the moment** you hold their food.
- They **stop the moment** you put it away.
- No delay, and no animals that keep trailing you after the food is gone.

Reliable Tempt replaces *No Animal Tempt Delay*, so don't install both. It needs no library mod.
